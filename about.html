About Us

The story, purpose and promise behind Adventure Escape SA.

Our history

Adventure Escape SA was established in 2024 and operates throughout the Western Cape. The business began as an initiative by Liam Daniels, who has always had a passion for outdoor adventure and eco-tourism.

Liam noticed that many families, tourists, schools and corporate groups struggled to find one place where they could compare and book exciting outdoor activities. Adventure Escape SA brings together a variety of guided outdoor experiences that encourage teamwork, fitness and a love of nature.

Vision, mission and goals

Our vision

To be the Western Cape’s most trusted home for guided outdoor adventure, where every visitor discovers the beauty of the outdoors.

Our mission

To deliver safe, professionally guided and unforgettable outdoor experiences that build teamwork, fitness and a lasting love of nature.

Our goals

  • Make comparing and booking adventures simple
  • Keep every experience safe and professionally guided
  • Reward customers who book more with loyalty discounts
  • Protect the natural spaces we explore

What makes us different

Guided and safe

Professional guides, safety briefings and equipment on every experience.

For everyone

Individuals, families, schools, tourists and corporate groups are all welcome.

Eco-minded

Experiences designed to appreciate and protect the landscape.

Rewarding

Up to 15% off when you book more than three experiences.
